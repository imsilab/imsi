Hyunsu Go

Undergraduate Intern

IMSI Research Lab, Seoul National University

Hyunsu Go is an undergraduate student at Seoul National University studying Mechanical Engineering and Computer Science and Engineering, with an additional interest in economics. As an Undergraduate Intern at IMSI Research Lab, he works at the intersection of medical imaging and machine learning.

He contributes to the SUDAL and PNI teams, developing learning-based methods for 3D MRI analysis and perineural invasion prediction. His research experience includes self-supervised representation learning, adaptive neural architectures, diffusion-based imaging, and clinically grounded evaluation of medical AI systems.

Research Interests

  • 3D Medical Image Analysis
  • Perineural Invasion Prediction
  • Self-Supervised Learning
  • Diffusion and State-Space Models
  • Multimodal Medical AI
