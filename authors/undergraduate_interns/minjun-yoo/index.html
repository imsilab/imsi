Minjun Yoo

Undergraduate Intern

IMSI Research Lab, Seoul National University

Minjun Yoo is an undergraduate student in Mathematics Education at Seoul National University (Class of 2023) and an Undergraduate Intern at IMSI Research Lab. After completing his mandatory military service, he returned to his studies as a second-year student and continues to combine mathematical thinking, public data, and practical software development.

Building Cooler Routes with Geuneulro

Minjun created Geuneulro, a shade-aware navigation app that helps pedestrians find more comfortable routes during hot weather. Built rapidly from public data, the app drew nationwide attention for translating an everyday problem into an accessible mobile service.
