Eunseob Choi

Undergraduate Intern

IMSI Research Lab, Seoul National University

Eunseob Choi is an undergraduate student in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST) and an Undergraduate Intern at IMSI Research Lab. He contributes to the SUDAL team, where he explores robust and data-efficient learning for medical imaging.

His work spans self-supervised representation learning, medical image reconstruction and analysis, vision-language reasoning, and generative modeling. Through projects on masked autoencoders, state-space models, and GPU-accelerated clinical AI pipelines, he studies how modern representation learning can support practical biomedical applications.

Research Interests

  • Medical Image Analysis
  • Self-Supervised Representation Learning
  • Medical Image Reconstruction
  • Vision-Language Models
  • Generative AI
